How to export from Gorgias

  1. 1

    In Gorgias, open Inbox and click All under Default views. To check only the AI Agent's tickets, use Shared views › AI Agent › All instead.

  2. 2

    Tick the checkbox at the top of the ticket list so it says All selected. If Gorgias offers to select every ticket in the view, take that too.

  3. 3

    Click the three dots next to it, then Export tickets. Gorgias emails you a link to the CSV; it works for 14 days.

    What this can't tell you

    • Whether the refund, reship or 3PL change actually happened. That lives in Shopify, your 3PL and your carrier, not in the ticket.
    • Exact categories. It's a keyword estimate; check a few tickets per category yourself.
    • What the leftovers cost you. That needs your own handle times and hourly cost.
